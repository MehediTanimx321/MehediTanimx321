## About me

I am pursuing a master's degree in **Electrical & Microsystems Engineering at OTH Regensburg**, Germany. I hold a bachelor's degree in **Electrical & Electronics Engineering** from Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University (HSTU), Bangladesh.

My interests include **semiconductor devices, photovoltaics, microsystems, electronic circuits, electrical engineering R&D, and device simulation**. I have explored lead-free perovskite solar cells using **SCAPS-1D and MATLAB**.

Previously, I worked as an Electrical Engineer at **Quality Feeds Limited** (2025) and completed engineering training at **Bangladesh Power Development Board (BPDB)** (2024).

## Research highlights

- **Lead-free Cs₂TiBr₆ solar cells:** investigated kesterite-based hole transport layers and dual hole transport layer architectures.
- **K₂TiI₆ photovoltaics:** simulation-based exploration of device performance using SCAPS-1D.
- **Dual-absorber architectures:** research into perovskite/kesterite combinations and transport-layer materials.
